📌 Supplier Management

The Supplier Management module maintains supplier information such as supplier ID, supplier name, contact details, email and the items supplied. A supplier can supply multiple items. The system counts each item separately and displays the number of items supplied by each supplier. This information helps the warehouse identify suitable suppliers during procurement.
